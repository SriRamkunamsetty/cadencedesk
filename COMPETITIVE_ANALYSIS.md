## 3. What Separates 9.5–10/10 Winners From the Rest of the Field

1. **Honest SDK 3.5.0 Engineering Over Feature Bloat:** Top entries (*Heart on Stream*, *Upbeat*, *FlowBreak*, *Witness*, *Feltbeat*, *Drift*, *Glint*) explicitly solve real SDK hardware quirks:
   - **SDK 3.5.0 automatic LLM Insights check-in:** *Feltbeat*, *Heart on Stream*, and *Witness* independently discovered that SDK 3.5.0 registers an automatic `insight` event (~once/min) even when unrequested. Disclosing this transparently on the consent screen and discarding payloads unread is now the gold standard.
   - **Warm-up & confidence gating:** Top apps never display raw uncalibrated metrics. They enforce `stable === true`, confidence floors (`>= 40` pulse, `>= 45` breathing, `>= 50` HRV), freshness (`< 5 s`), and turn warm-up windows into purposeful UX (e.g., *Upbeat*'s tuning hum, *Long Exposure*'s silver-plate sitting).
   - **Real failure-mode guards:** `face.talking` pauses breathing scoring (*Readback*, *Exhale*, *Breathe as One*, *Prelude*); 2nd-face `validationStatus` pauses or aborts capture (*Heart on Stream*, *Long Exposure*, *Glint*); 640×480 @ 30 fps prevents indoor FPS drops below 25 fps (*MDCAT Focus Desk*, *Drift*); pre-flight FPS check protects free-trial credits (*LIFEFORM*, *Witness*).
   - **Known SDK field realities:** `breathing.apnea` is unpopulated in SDK 3.5.0 (*Breathmarks*); `face.blinking` misses sustained 3 s eye closures while `face.landmarks` EAR catches 10/10 (*Drift*); protobuf `int64 Long` timestamps need safe conversion (*FlowBreak*); `destroy()` vs. `stop()` avoids 30 s hang on exit (*Prelude*).
2. **Real Automated Tests & Live Demo Proof:** Winning contenders ship 16 to 1,100+ unit tests on their pure-function signal/state engines and record **live webcam footage** (never simulator-only screenshots).
3. **Zero Medical Claims & Main-Process Consent Enforcement:** Every top entry avoids diagnostic/clinical terms and blocks camera acquisition in Electron's main process until explicit per-session consent is granted.
